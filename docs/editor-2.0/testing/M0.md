# M0 manual test checklist (WP 0.2: editor mode and camera)

Setup: Minecraft 26.1.1 with Fabric, Fabric API and the Litematica Array jar (it bundles cg-uihost). Litematica itself is not needed for these tests. Test in a creative singleplayer world, then once in survival, then once in spectator (`/gamemode spectator`).

Tick each line. Anything that fails: note what you did, what you expected and what happened.

## Enter and exit
- [ ] Controls > Key Binds shows a "Litematica Array" category with "Toggle editor mode", default F6.
- [ ] F6 in the world: the cursor appears (free), the top toolbar shows the "EDITOR  F6" chip, the status bar appears at the bottom with the hints, the crosshair is gone, the hand is gone, and your own player model is visible from behind.
- [ ] The view at the moment of entering is the same as the player's view (no jump).
- [ ] F6 again: the toolbar and status bar go away, the camera is back in your eyes, the mouse is grabbed again and mouse look works.
- [ ] Click the "EDITOR F6" chip: same as F6 (leaves, mouse grabbed again). Hovering it shows "Leave the editor (F6)".
- [ ] Rebind the key to something else (for example G), leave and re-enter with it. It must work in editor mode too (the rebound key is not blocked).
- [ ] The third-person setting you had before (F5) is restored after leaving.

## Esc
- [ ] In editor mode, Esc does nothing: no pause menu. Only F6 (or the chip) leaves.
- [ ] Outside editor mode Esc still opens the pause menu.

## Orbit, pan, zoom
- [ ] Hold the middle mouse button on empty space and drag: the cursor disappears, the view orbits around a point in front of you. Dragging right makes the near scene move right, dragging down moves it down. Pitch stops before straight up/down.
- [ ] Release: the cursor comes back at the spot where you pressed.
- [ ] Shift + middle drag pans (the scene follows the cursor). Pressing or releasing Shift during the drag switches between orbit and pan without a jump.
- [ ] Wheel zooms 12 % per notch. The block under the cursor stays under the cursor while zooming (zoom toward the cursor's ray). Zooming out far and back in works, it never gets stuck at the target.
- [ ] Motion is smooth (a short ease-in/out), also at low and very high frame rates, and does not depend on the game being at 20 TPS.
- [ ] Numpad . and F frame your player (the camera moves so the player's box fills the view). The view direction is kept.

## Fly
- [ ] Hold the right mouse button and move the mouse: the cursor disappears and the view turns around the camera.
- [ ] While RMB is held: W/A/S/D move, Q goes down, E goes up, Shift is about 3 times faster.
- [ ] Wheel while flying changes the speed (0.2x to 8x). A pill under the toolbar shows "Fly", a speed bar and the multiplier; it is gone when RMB is released. The status bar switches to the fly hints.
- [ ] Hold RMB without moving for a moment (about a quarter second): fly starts (cursor hides).
- [ ] Hold RMB and press W without moving the mouse: fly starts at once.
- [ ] Release RMB: the cursor comes back where it was.
- [ ] A quick right click (under 250 ms, mouse still): nothing visible happens yet (the context menu comes later), the cursor never hides for more than a blink, and fly does not start. A right press with a 3 px wobble that is released quickly also counts as a click; a 6 px move starts fly.

## Vanilla input is blocked
- [ ] In editor mode W/A/S/D, Space, Shift, Ctrl do not move the player (check from a second window or by leaving: the player stays put).
- [ ] Left click and right click on the world do not break or place blocks, and do not re-grab the mouse.
- [ ] Number keys 1-9, the wheel (when not over a window), Q (drop), F (swap hands) and E (inventory) do nothing vanilla in editor mode. E does not open the inventory.
- [ ] Hold W, press F6, release W later: the player does not keep walking after leaving.
- [ ] Mouse movement never turns the player, also while orbiting or flying.

## Chat and function keys
- [ ] T opens chat and you can type; `/` opens chat with a slash. The editor stays on behind it, the cursor stays free.
- [ ] Send a message or press Esc in chat: chat closes, the mouse is NOT grabbed, editor mode is still on.
- [ ] F3 toggles the debug screen. F1 hides the HUD. F2 takes a screenshot. F11 toggles fullscreen. F5 does not bring back the first-person camera.
- [ ] F6 while chat is open does nothing (typing keys go to chat). Close chat, then F6 leaves.
- [ ] Hold RMB (fly) and press T: chat opens, fly stops and the cursor returns.

## Auto-exit
- [ ] `/gamemode` to the same mode, no change. Change the dimension (nether portal or `/execute in minecraft:the_nether run tp @s ~ ~ ~`): editor mode ends.
- [ ] Disconnect or leave the world while in editor mode: editor mode is off when you join again; the mouse is normal in the menus.
- [ ] Something opens a screen other than chat (for example the window loses focus and Minecraft pauses, or a command opens a screen): editor mode ends and the screen works normally, with a free cursor.
- [ ] Alt-tab away and back in editor mode: the mouse is not grabbed, editor mode still on.

## Spectator
- [ ] In spectator mode, enter the editor and fly 200+ blocks away: the player entity follows the camera (see F3 coordinates), chunks load around the camera. The "Fly" pill shows "loading chunks..." while the chunk under the camera has not arrived.
- [ ] Leave the editor: you are where the camera was, in first person.
- [ ] In creative and survival the player stays where it was while the camera flies (check with F3 or by leaving the editor: you are back at your own position).

## Layout
- [ ] Change the GUI scale and the window size: toolbar stays centred at the top, status bar stays full width at the bottom. The hints are dropped from the end when the window is narrow.
- [ ] No Dear ImGui demo window anywhere (Ctrl+F6 does nothing any more).
- [ ] With Manhattan installed too: known conflict until WP 6.4 (both claim the GLFW callbacks). Test without Manhattan.
